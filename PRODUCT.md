# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Property owners, managers, and staff running PGs, hostels, co-living spaces, and student housing. They need real-time visibility into occupancy, revenue, complaints, and operations. Primary context: daily dashboard checks on desktop/mobile, quick status scans between meetings.

## Product Purpose

Opsora is an operating system for PG/hostel management — handling residents, rooms/beds, rent payments with proof verification, complaints/tickets, food polls, IoT water/electricity monitoring, and staff workflows. Success means zero manual spreadsheets, instant property visibility, and streamlined tenant operations.

## Brand Personality

Professional, precise, quietly confident. Not warm-and-fuzzy, not aggressive and loud. Competent and trustworthy.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS admin templates with bland card grids
- Gradient-heavy, glassmorphism, animated hero sections
- Warm cream/sand/beige backgrounds
- Over-designed marketing aesthetics on operational tools

## Design Principles

1. Information density and visual clarity are inseparable
2. Typography hierarchy through weight and size, never through color alone
3. Color communicates status, not mood
4. Surfaces are flat at rest; shadows appear only on interaction
5. Every element earns its place through hierarchy, not decoration

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Reduced motion alternatives for all animations. 44px minimum touch targets on mobile. High contrast for data readability.
