# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Three equally important roles in PG/hostel/co-living management:

- **Property Owners/Operators** — Manage multiple properties, track occupancy and revenue, resolve complaints, monitor IoT (water/electricity). Context: desktop-first, daily operational use.
- **Staff** — Handle day-to-day tasks, check-ins, complaint resolution, food management. Context: mix of desktop and mobile, task-oriented workflows.
- **Residents** — Pay rent, submit complaints, view food menus, manage profile. Context: mobile-first, occasional interaction, expect consumer-grade UX.

## Product Purpose

Opsora is the operating system for PGs, hostels, co-living spaces, and student housing. It replaces fragmented spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, and manual tracking with a unified platform for occupancy management, rent collection, complaint handling, IoT monitoring, and food planning.

Success looks like: owners making data-driven decisions faster, staff completing tasks with less friction, and residents having a transparent, easy-to-use portal that reduces back-and-forth communication.

## Brand Personality

**Modern, Smart, Reliable**

- Voice: competent, direct, no-nonsense — the software is confident without being flashy
- Tone: professional and efficient, like a well-run property — not a consumer app
- Emotional goal: trust. The user should feel "this handles my business properly"

## Anti-references

- **Over-designed / flashy**: No gradients, glassmorphism, animated hero sections, or decorative motion. Opsora is a tool, not a marketing page.
- **Generic SaaS admin**: No Bootstrap-like templates, bland card grids with icon + heading + text, or cookie-cutter dashboard layouts. Every surface should feel intentional.
- **Enterprise/corporate**: No dense unreadable tables, cluttered navigation with 20+ items, or overwhelming data dumps. Clarity over density.
- **Warm cream/beige AI defaults**: Avoid the saturated warm-neutral monoculture (OKLCH L 0.84-0.97, C < 0.06). Opsora's canvas should be a true neutral or slightly cool tinted gray, not sand/parchment.

## Design Principles

1. **Data-first hierarchy** — Information density with absolute clarity. Every number, status, and label earns its place through hierarchy, not decoration.
2. **Professional restraint** — No visual element exists without purpose. White space, typography weight, and alignment are the design system — not color, shadow, or border.
3. **Consistent, intentional typography** — Hierarchy is built through weight, size, and spacing — never through color alone. Readability at every viewport.
4. **Smart efficiency** — Every interaction should feel fast and intelligent. Minimal clicks, clear affordances, predictable patterns across all three portals.
5. **Quiet confidence** — The interface should feel like a well-run operation: organized, trustworthy, and competent without being loud about it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG AAA preferred** — 7:1 contrast ratio for body text, 4.5:1 for large text
- Full keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators
- Screen reader support with semantic HTML and ARIA labels
- Reduced motion support: every animation must have a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative
- Touch targets minimum 44px for mobile resident portal
- Color never used as the sole indicator of status or meaning
